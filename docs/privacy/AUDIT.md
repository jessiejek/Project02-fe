## Endpoint review (PV-2)

Method: listed every action in `BE: src/ClinicApp.Api/Controllers/*.cs` with its `[Authorize]` attributes, then read each action that takes an id or returns patient data.

* **Anonymous on purpose (no patient data):** `GET settings`, `GET services[/id]`, `GET doctors[/id|/schedules|/blocked-dates|/day-status(es)|/services]` (license/PTR/S2 and staff contact details are redacted for anonymous and patient callers — `DoctorsController.cs:94`), `GET announcements`, `GET admin/operating-hours`, `GET admin/payment-methods`, `GET doctor-services`, lookups (medicines, ICD-10, vitals templates, lab catalog), `GET version`, `/health`, and the auth endpoints (login/register/refresh/forgot/reset/…; rate-limited, `AuthController.cs:22`).
* **Development only:** `POST dev/import` returns 404 outside Development (`DevController.cs`).
* **Admin only:** staff invites/delete, services/settings/announcement writes, refunds, audit search, access list, data-request queue, doctor admin lists.
* **Staff-like (Admin/Staff/Doctor):** patient list/search/create/allergies/lab values, queue, payments, reports (per `ReportsController`).
* **Patient-scoped:** bookings, consultations, prescriptions, documents, lab results, vitals, vaccinations, reviews, payments are filtered or refused to the patient's own rows (`IdorTests`, 40+ cases).
* **Doctor-scoped writes:** consultations, prescriptions, follow-ups, lab orders, certificates, templates, queue moves, payment waivers check `ActsAsDoctor` (the doctor's own work, or Admin).
* Client-written audit rows are refused (410) so the trail can't be forged — `AuditLogsController.cs:97`.
