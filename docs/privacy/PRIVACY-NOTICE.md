## Who we are
Dr. Grace Gavino Medical Clinic ("the clinic") runs this patient system. The clinic decides what patient information is collected and why (the "personal information controller").

## What we collect
- **Identity and contact:** name, date of birth, sex, address, phone, email, emergency contact.
- **Health information:** visit notes, diagnoses, prescriptions, vital signs, lab orders and results, vaccinations, allergies, medical certificates, and files you or the clinic upload.
- **Booking and payment records:** appointments, fees, payment method, proof of payment.
- **Account and security records:** login email, sign-in times, failed sign-in attempts, and a record of which clinic staff opened your record.

## Why we collect it
1. To give you medical care and keep an accurate medical record.
2. To schedule appointments, issue receipts and certificates, and handle billing.
3. To keep the system secure and to show who accessed your record.
4. To meet legal duties such as keeping medical records.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who can see it
Only clinic personnel who need it for their work: your doctor, front-desk staff and the clinic administrator. Every time they open your record the system writes it down, and the administrator can review that list. We share information with outside parties only when you ask us to (for example a laboratory or an HMO) or when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it
Medical records are kept for at least **7 years after your last visit**, then deleted or anonymised. Account and security logs are kept only as long as needed to protect the system. *(Clinic to confirm the retention period with its adviser.)*

## How we protect it
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected sign-in cookies.
- Passwords are stored hashed; accounts lock after repeated wrong passwords; passwords must be at least 12 characters.
- Role-based access: patients see only their own record.
- Uploaded files are never public; they open only for signed-in people who are allowed to see them.

## Your rights
- **To be informed** — this notice and any change to it.
- **To access** — download a copy of your data in your profile (Privacy tab).
- **To correct** — ask us to fix wrong information (Privacy tab → Request a correction).
- **To erasure or blocking** — ask us to delete your data. Medical records must be kept for 7 years after your last visit, so the request is logged and carried out when that period ends.
- **To object or withdraw consent** — at any time from the Privacy tab. Withdrawing does not undo what was lawfully done before, and the clinic may be unable to book new visits until you agree again.
- **To complain** — to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

## If something goes wrong
If personal data is breached in a way that could harm you, the clinic will tell the National Privacy Commission and the people affected within **72 hours** of knowing about it.

## Changes to this notice
When the notice changes in a way that matters, the clinic publishes a new consent version and every patient is asked to review and decide again. Each of your decisions is kept with its date and version.
